German-English Tech Grammar Bridge

Master German Nebensatz (Subordinate Clause) verb-final positioning in incident communication:

Rule: Subordinating conjunctions (weil, da, wenn, damit) push conjugated verbs to the very end. DE: "...weil der Pod wegen OOMKilled neu gestartet wurde." EN: "...because the pod was restarted due to OOMKilled." DevOps tip: In Jira tickets & post-mortems, auxiliary verbs always land at clause finish!
